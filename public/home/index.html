About Architex

Built for a chain where gas is a dollar

On Arc, USDC is the gas token. There is no wrapped native coin and no guessing what a fee costs. Architex is designed around that: every pool is priced in dollars, every fee is paid in dollars, and every number you see is read straight from the chain.

The interface is intentionally quiet. Big numbers, clear routes, one button that moves money. You should be able to go from landing to a confirmed swap in under a minute.

Instant quotes

Prices are computed locally from pool reserves, so the screen never waits on the network to answer a keystroke.

Dollar native

USDC in, USDC out. Swaps, launch fees and creator fees are all settled in the currency you already hold.

Honest by default

No invented TVL, no fake volume. Every figure links back to the explorer and every contract source is public.

Technology

Designed to work together

Core AMM

A constant-product x·y=k exchange where the pair contract is the LP token.

  • Factory, Pair, Router, Lens
  • EIP-2612 permit exits
  • Multi-hop through USDC

Launchpad

Every token gets the same fair curve: 1B supply, 800M on the curve, 200M seeded into its launch pool.

  • Creator fee 0 to 10%, locked
  • Fees accrue, never pushed
  • Graduates at 25,000 USDC raised

Plugins

An open ERC-165 interface so creator fees can flow into any logic, from simple splits to deeper liquidity.

  • Split, Holder rewards, Combo
  • Deepen pool
  • Custom addresses allowed
